# 🎯 Class 12 Board Prep — Study Group & Weightage Tracker

A minimal, high-efficiency web app designed specifically for **12th Class students (BIEAP Andhra Pradesh Board)** preparing for Board Exams together, optimized for Android devices.

---

## 📱 Android Device Access
Open immediately on your phone (connected to the same Wi-Fi):
- **On Android Phones:** `http://192.168.1.6:3000` *(or `http://192.168.1.6:3001`)*
- **On your Computer:** [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) *(or [http://localhost:3001](http://localhost:3001))*

> **Install as an Android App:** On Android Chrome, tap `⋮` (menu) > **"Add to Home screen"** to get a clean full-screen native app experience!

---

## 🌟 Latest Updates:

### 1. 📐 Mathematics: Exercise-Based Task Creator
- **No Mark Confusion:** For Mathematics, instead of 1M/2M/4M/8M, you now select the specific **Exercise** from the official BIEAP syllabus:
  - *Integrals:* Exercise 7(a) through 7(j), Chapter 7 MCQs
  - *Determinants:* Exercise 4(a) through 4(f), Chapter 4 MCQs
  - *Continuity & Differentiability:* Exercise 5(a) through 5(h), Chapter 5 MCQs
  - *Vector Algebra:* Exercise 10(a) through 10(e), Chapter 10 MCQs
  - *And all other 13 Mathematics chapters!*
- For **Physics** and **Chemistry**, you select the **Question Types (1M, 2M, 4M, 8M)** matching their exam blueprint.

### 2. ✅ "End Task" Dialog with Completed Questions Logging
- When creating a task, you **no longer need to pick number of questions upfront**!
- When you finish studying and tap **"End Task"** (or the checkbox), a clean dialog asks:
  - **"All Questions"** (completed the entire set or exercise in one tap)
  - **"Custom Number"** (enter exact count e.g. 5, 8, 12 questions solved)
- Logged directly on the task card: `✓ Completed: All Questions` or `✓ Completed: 8 Questions`.

### 3. 👥 Group Task Creator
- Check **"Create Task for ALL Members"** to assign a Maths exercise or Science question set to your entire study circle simultaneously.

### 4. 📊 Dual Progress Bars & Study Timer
- **Entire Group Bar:** Collective daily completion % + overall board syllabus mastery %.
- **Member Progress Bar:** Individual daily to-dos + personal syllabus marks.
- **Built-in Focus Timer:** Pomodoro 25m, 15m, 45m, 60m, or Stopwatch linked directly to today's tasks.
